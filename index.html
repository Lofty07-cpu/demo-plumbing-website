GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Are the prices on this page fixed?

No. The prices are fictional examples for this demo. A real business should publish only verified rates and confirm the scope, parts and any call-out charge before work is approved.

Do you cover my area?

This sample site has no real service area. Replace “your area” with the locations your business actually serves and confirm availability when enquiries come in.

What should I include when asking for an estimate?

Describe the issue, share your suburb or area, explain when it started and mention any access constraints. Photos can help with an initial discussion, but may not replace an on-site assessment.

Can I book emergency plumbing?

Emergency availability is not configured on this demo website. Add your real hours and emergency contact details only if your business can provide that service.
